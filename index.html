LoFi YouTube Bot

A local production and channel-management tool for Deep Mode Session, controlled by its owner through Telegram.

The tool prepares instrumental music videos, brings audio and visual previews together for review, and uses YouTube API Services to upload approved videos and manage their metadata and publication schedules.

01 / PREPARE

Produce locally

Music, visual assets and project records are prepared and stored on the operator’s computer and connected drives.

02 / REVIEW

Review the session

The owner reviews audio, visuals, thumbnails and video details through the Telegram workflow.

03 / PUBLISH

Manage the channel

Approved uploads are sent to YouTube. Metadata, upload status and publication schedules are managed through the authorized connection.

Google account access

The application requests Google authorization in the browser. It does not request your Google password. Authorization permits reading channel and video information, uploading videos, and managing authorized YouTube content.

This website provides application information only. It does not provide a login form, run the bot, or receive OAuth credentials. The application is intended for its owner’s channels, not as a public account-management service.

Operator and channel

The application is operated by the GitHub account Thegreatmen for Deep Mode Session. It is an independent tool and is not endorsed by Google, YouTube, Telegram or GitHub.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before authorizing or using the application. The support email is displayed on the Google consent screen; public documentation questions can be submitted through the site’s issue tracker.
